# Why Royal B missed the MU / TSM / GOOGL bounce (Nov 12, 2026)

## What happened
- **Nov 5:** chip and memory stocks had their worst one-day sell-off since 2020 (AI sell-off; Kioxia warned NAND price gains are slowing). MU -17.5%, AMD -17.3%, TSM -10.8%, AVGO -10.4%, all on 2–4x normal volume.
- **Nov 6 on:** rebound on news: memory demand returning, analyst calls, and a report that TSMC could make memory chips for Nvidia (TSM +6%). MU had its best day since July and got back above $800 on Nov 11.
- **Nov 12 (midday prices):** from the Nov 5 low, MU +21%, TSM +16%, AVGO +25%, AMD +44%, SMH +13%. GOOGL +8% from its Nov 2 low.
- **Nov 12:** NVDA -8 to -12% after Jensen Huang resigned as CEO (news shock, not a chart signal).

## Why no screen fired
- **Trend Cross** needs the price above a rising 50-day: all were below it.
- **Early Turn pullback** needs a dip *to* the 20-day that holds: these fell straight through it.
- **Early Turn breakout** needs a new 3-month high: far away.
- **Comebacks / Comeback Turn** need a stock 25%+ below its 2-year high *and lagging the S&P*: MU, TSM, AMD were big winners, so they don't qualify; the Turn also needs a close back above the 20-day.
- **Nothing reads news,** so the "why" (memory demand, TSMC report, upgrades) never reached Royal B.

The gap: **a leader that gets shaken out hard, then turns.** That is the Shakeout Bounce detector in PLAN.md, plus the Sector Rebound detector (the whole chip group turned on Nov 6).
